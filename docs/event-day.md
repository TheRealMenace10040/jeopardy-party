# Event day checklist

App: **https://jeopardy-party-five.vercel.app**

## The day before

- [ ] On your phone, open `/host`, sign in with Google, and check your game is in **Your games** with no "not finished" note.
- [ ] Do a 2-minute test: tap **Play**, open `/tv` on the laptop, join from a second phone, buzz once. Then go back to Your games (the test room just expires).
- [ ] Charge the host phone and the laptop. Pack the HDMI cable (and a USB-C/HDMI adapter if the laptop needs one).

## Setting up (about 5 minutes)

1. **Laptop → TV:** plug in HDMI, set the TV to that input. If the laptop shows its desktop on the TV, you're good; if it mirrors at a weird size, choose "Duplicate" or "Second screen only" (Windows: press `Win + P`).
2. **Host phone:** open `/host` → your game → **Play**. You'll see a 4-character room code.
3. **Laptop:** open **jeopardy-party-five.vercel.app/tv**, type the room code, press **F11** for full screen. The TV now shows the QR code and room code.
4. Turn the laptop's sleep/screensaver off for the evening (Windows: Settings → System → Power → Screen "Never").
5. Make sure the venue Wi-Fi or mobile data works on the laptop and your phone. Guests can use their own mobile data.

## Starting the game

1. Guests scan the QR code on the TV (or go to the site and enter the code), type a team name. Up to 6 teams.
2. On your phone, choose **Who picks first?** and tap **Start the game**.

## Running each clue (all on your phone)

1. The picking team tells you a category and value → tap that square.
2. Read the clue aloud → tap **Open buzzers**.
3. Your phone shows who buzzed first. They say their answer → tap **✓ Correct** or **✗ Incorrect**.
   - Wrong: buzzers reopen for the other teams automatically.
   - Nobody knows: tap **Nobody got it: show the answer**.
4. Tap **Back to the board**.
- **Daily Double:** the team bets on their phone; then read the clue and judge. (If their phone acts up, type their bet on yours.)
- **Mistake?** Tap **Undo**, or tap a team's score to set it.

## Final Jeopardy

1. When the board is done, tap **Go to Final Jeopardy**. Teams bet secretly on their phones.
2. Tap **Reveal the final clue** → 30-second timer; teams type their responses (+10 seconds button if needed).
3. Tap **Start the reveal** and judge each team ✓/✗ (lowest score first), then **Show final standings**.

## If something goes wrong

- **A team's phone locked or refreshed:** they reopen the same link; they rejoin as the same team with their score.
- **The TV page froze or Wi-Fi dropped:** refresh the `/tv` page; it reconnects to the same room.
- **Your phone refreshed or died:** sign in on any device at `/host` → **Games in progress → Rejoin as host**.
- **A score is wrong:** tap that team's score on your phone and type the right number.
